Set-up: OpenSim master with WebRTC voice working (spatial voice can be heard in the region), and a viewer that
uses WebRTC voice (tested with Firestorm 7.2).

P2P call (needs two avatars, both on the same simulator):
1. Avatar A opens an IM with avatar B and presses "Call".
2. Expected: B sees an incoming-call dialog; when B accepts, A and B can talk.
   Actual: A's call ends almost at once. B sees nothing.
   A's viewer log shows: "Received invalid credentials for channel ...".

Group call (one avatar is enough):
1. Open a group's chat window and press "Call" (or "Join Call").
2. Expected: you join the group's voice call.
   Actual: the call ends at once, with the same "invalid credentials" line in the viewer log.

Declined call (only with the patch applied, since without it the callee is never rung):
1. A calls B as above. B presses "Decline" (or lets the 30-second dialog run out).
2. Expected: A's viewer says the call was declined and ends it.
   Without the patch's decline handling, A would keep "calling" until hanging up.
