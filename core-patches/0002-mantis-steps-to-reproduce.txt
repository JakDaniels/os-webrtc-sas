Set-up: OpenSim master, physics = ubODE, NPCs enabled ([NPC] Enabled = true), OSSL NPC functions allowed.

1. Stand on flat ground in the middle of a region, e.g. at <128,128>.
2. In a prim, run: osNpcCreate("Test", "NPC", llGetPos() + <2,0,0>, llGetOwner());
3. Watch the NPC for a few seconds (or log osNpcGetPos every 0.2 s).

Expected: the NPC stays where it was made.
Actual: about a second later it jumps towards the region's corner <0,0>: its X and Y become about 0.9 of what they
were (from <130,128> to about <117,115>), then it settles there. It does not happen every time; it depends on the
NPC's body going to sleep before its first movement update.
