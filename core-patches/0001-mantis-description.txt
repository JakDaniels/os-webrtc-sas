With WebRTC voice, group voice calls and person-to-person (P2P) voice calls do not work. Spatial voice (region
and parcel) works fine.

What happens:
- When you start a call (from a group chat, or from an IM with another avatar), the viewer asks the region for
  the call's voice channel. OpenSim answers "OK" but sends no channel, so the viewer ends the call straight away.
- The person you call is never told that someone is calling them.
- If the person called declines, the caller is not told and keeps "calling".

The attached patch fixes all three. It changes one file:
OpenSim/Addons/os-webrtc-janus/WebRtcVoiceRegionModule/WebRtcVoiceRegionModule.cs

With the patch:
- The region answers the viewer's "call" request with the call's voice channel, so the call connects.
- For a P2P call, the person called gets the normal incoming-call dialog (if they are on the same simulator).
- If they decline, the caller's viewer shows "declined" and ends the call.
- Only people who belong in a call can get or join its channel: members of the group for a group call, and the
  two avatars for a P2P call.

Spatial voice is not changed. The patch applies cleanly to current master (9d00a55) with: git apply -p1
